Learn The History of Space Exploration

Space exploration has evolved from ancient astronomical observations to human and robotic missions across the solar system, driven by curiosity, technology, and international competition.

Human fascination with the cosmos dates back thousands of years. Ancient civilizations in Mesopotamia, China, Greece, and the Americas developed sophisticated methods to track celestial bodies, create star charts, predict eclipses, and align monuments with astronomical events. The invention of the telescope in the early 17th century, notably used by Galileo Galilei, revealed Jupiter's moons and the phases of Venus, providing evidence for the heliocentric model and laying the groundwork for modern astronomy.

Learn The Fundamentals Of The Solar System And Much More..

The solar system consists of the Sun and all celestial bodies that orbit it, including eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets, all bound by the Sun's gravity.

Understanding the solar system is crucial for comprehending the origins of Earth and the conditions that support life. It also provides insights into the processes that govern planetary formation and evolution, which can be applied to the study of exoplanets and other planetary systems beyond our own.
